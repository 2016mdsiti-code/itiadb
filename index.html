FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of projects do you take on?

We focus on modern websites, product pages, and high-quality 3D heavy visuals for creative teams and digital products.

How does the process usually work?

Most projects ship in Framer. For specific project needs, we combine Figma, Framer, and light custom code carefully.

Which tools do you use to build sites?

Most projects ship in Framer. For specific project needs, we combine Figma, Framer, and light custom code.

How long does a typical project take?

Small landing pages take 1–2 weeks. Larger, multi page sites usually need 3–6 weeks.

Can you use our existing branding?

Yes. We can plug into your current brand system or refine it while we build the site.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We can handle small updates, new sections, and ongoing design tweaks through simple monthly support plans.
